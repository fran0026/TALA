CDM System.

A digital system designed to support commodity delivery operations through structured workflows, tracking and operational visibility.

OperationsVehicle ManagementJourney TrackingInformation Systems

The project

Commodity delivery operations.

CDM System brings commodity delivery activity into a structured digital workspace, supporting transport requests, vehicle assignment, journey status, issue visibility and management oversight.

What it does

Built around the real workflow.

Vehicle managementOrganizes vehicle records, capacities, drivers, transporters and current vehicle status.
Transport requestsStructures delivery requests and assignment information around operational needs.
Journey trackingProvides visibility into vehicle movement, journey status and delivery progress.
Issue monitoringRecords reported transport issues and keeps their status visible to the responsible teams.
Transport databaseBrings transport records, filters, timelines and operational information into one workspace.
Management visibilitySummarizes operational activity through dashboards, status views and resource statistics.

Meet CDM System

Real CDM System interfaces presented without changing their underlying structure, so the operational workflow remains visible and understandable.
