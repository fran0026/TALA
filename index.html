My Sincere Message Francis Majisu · CEO

“I suffer deeply whenever I hear about the insecurity of people and systems in this rapidly evolving technological world. As technology continues to expand beyond imagination, I believe it must grow together with strong morals, faith, responsibility, and humanity.

It pains me to see people fear the very technology that was meant to empower and improve lives. My greatest desire is to see every person experience technology safely, freely, and without compromise, where innovation protects humanity instead of threatening it.

I believe this is one of the greatest responsibilities and powers God (Allah) has entrusted to us: to use knowledge, intelligence, and technology not for destruction or exploitation, but for protection, empowerment, justice, and the betterment of society.”

Francis Masanja Majisu is a Tanzanian technologist and Information Systems student at The University Of Dodoma - UDOM-CIVE, still learning and building digital solutions that solve real problems. He created DatApp for institutional financial tracking, CDMS (Cotton Delivery Management System) currently running at Alliance Ginneries Limited - Simiyu, and is expanding in software, cloud, networking, cybersecurity, and data through programs such as Cisco Networking Academy and Huawei ICT Academy.

He has held student leadership roles, earned national recognition in Young Scientists Tanzania (third place, Computer and Technology), and leads TALA SYSTEMS with a single promise: technology that is secure, moral, and accessible. Read My Tech Journey for the full narrative.
